## Reading
- **Correct answers came from commentary, not law.** Big, well-covered reforms (the 2026 tax changes) are answered from VMI, KPMG and news. Anything that needs the article text itself fails, *including the unchanged control (Q8)*.
- **e-tar.lt was never retrieved.** The law-text sources were e-seimas drafts and amending laws, lrv.lt PDFs, and lrvalstybe's stale copy.
- **Version awareness is absent.** Q2 reported a 2026 change as "planned"; Q10 mixed up pending and in-force amendments.
- Long-tail definitions (Q5) are simply unreachable.

That is the gap the project targets: current article text, with versions, from a source that search engines find.
